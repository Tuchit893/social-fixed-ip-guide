## 📋 What This Tool Does

Services detect the location of a user based on their IP address. When you travel or change networks, your IP address shifts. Automated security systems see these changes as suspicious activity. This can lead to account bans or limited access.

This project offers a guide to set up a fixed connection. It directs your traffic through a static residential address. This makes your connection appear consistent to websites. You maintain the same digital identity regardless of your physical location.

## 🛡️ Configuring Your Proxy

The software uses a process called proxy chaining. This sends your data through multiple points to hide your real location.

1. Open the settings tab inside the application.
2. Enter the address provided by your residential proxy provider.
3. Input your username and password into the designated fields.
4. Select the American server location from the dropdown list.
5. Click the "Apply" button to save these changes.

Your connection is now routed through your residential proxy. You can verify this by visiting a website that shows your current IP address. The address displayed should match the one you configured in the application.

## 🔑 Security and Privacy

Maintaining a fixed IP helps protect your accounts from automated triggers. By choosing a residential proxy, your traffic appears to come from a home network. Most security systems trust residential IPs more than data center IPs. This significantly lowers the risk of shadow bans or verification requests.

Keep your username and password details private. Do not share these credentials with anyone. If you suspect your details leaked, log into your proxy provider portal and reset your keys immediately.

## 📈 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this software record my private data?**
No. This tool operates locally on your machine. It does not send your browsing history to any server.

**Can I use this for streaming services?**
This tool focuses on account security for AI and social media platforms. Some streaming services detect proxy usage and may limit access.

**Will this slow down my internet?**
Any proxy setup adds a small amount of latency to your connection. This is normal, as your data travels to the proxy server before reaching the website.

**Do I need a new VPS for every account?**
One VPS is enough to manage multiple accounts. You can configure different proxy ports for each service you access.

**What happens if my VPS crashes?**
Your internet connection will stop until you restart the VPS or the software. This is a safety feature to prevent your real IP address from being exposed.
